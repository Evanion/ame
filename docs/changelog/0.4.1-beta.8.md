## Azeroth Mercantile Exchange 0.4.1-beta.8

AME turns the auction house into a trading desk: price history, charts, signals, arbitrage, posting prices and your
own portfolio. It records every scan and search you run through the game's native auction house APIs, so it works
with the default auction house window and any scanning addon (it works well with addons like Auctionator); without
one, a Full scan button on the auction house window runs a full scan for you. It never scans on its own and needs
no desktop app.

### Everything in AME so far

**Price history**
- Records full scans (replicate, browse-summary and classic "get all" scans), item searches, sell-tab rechecks and
  category browsing.
- Separate history per market (realm, or ruleset on WoW Forever) and per auction house: Horde, Alliance and goblin.
- Compact storage: full detail for the last 48 hours, hourly prices for a week, daily history for 60 days, weekly
  points for a year.
- A Full scan button on the auction house window when no scanning addon is loaded (and /ame scan with or without
  one), with its progress beside it and a countdown to the next scan the game allows (one every 15 minutes).
- Optional one-time import of your existing Auctionator price history.
- Outrageous prices (e.g. a single listing at a 1000% markup) are kept out of the numbers.
- Gear with a random suffix ("of the Monkey", "of Magic") is tracked per suffix, since each sells at its own price,
  shown with its full name and its own stats, and searched for by its full name.

**Market screener**
- Every item with history, with market price, how it compares with its average (7 days by default), supply, sells
  per day, signal and an opportunity score; optional columns for the average, lowest price, 7-day change,
  volatility, data age and market phase (column chooser).
- Auction house categories and watchlists in a sidebar, name search, quality filter (grey junk hidden by default),
  price range (in gold, or silver and copper: "9s 50c") and signal filters, with removable filter chips and saved views. Every column sorts.
- Rows with data older than 3 days fade; hovering a row explains its signal.
- Data confidence bars on every item.
- Market selector for any auction house you have data for.
- Right-click any item in a list (Market, Arbitrage, Portfolio) for its menu: open the chart, search the auction
  house, watchlists, an alert, a chat link, or hide it.
- Hide items you never trade: they leave the Market, Arbitrage and Portfolio lists and their totals (AME keeps
  recording them), and wait under Hidden in the sidebar to be unhidden.

**Market overview** (switch it on in Settings, General)
- The whole auction house at a glance, like a stock exchange's indexes: a market index and a CPI (what players use
  up, weighted by what's spent on it), listed value, turnover, auctions and breadth, over the period you pick.
- Categories by share of the listed value (click one to open it in the screener), the biggest risers and fallers
  per category, and the items the total leans on most.
- Fair numbers: only items listed at least 3 at a time on most days move the indexes, and prices are capped at the
  outlier limit.

**Item charts**
- Price chart with market, lowest and 7-day average, or candlesticks with daily highs and lows (the Guide shows how
  to read them).
- 2D intraday view (every scan of the last two days), a 7D view with hourly prices, and 30D to All ranges.
- Two averages, as on a trading chart: MA 20 (the last 20 candles, so its span follows the range) and the fixed
  7-day average the signals compare against. Click any line in the legend to hide or show it.
- The vendor price as a dashed line when it's close to the auction house prices (otherwise noted in the legend).
- Your own purchases and sales as dots on the chart.
- Zoom, pan, value tags and a hover readout.
- Search by item name, Shift-click items while the Item tab is showing, or pick a modifier click in Settings.
- Hover the item's name or icon for the game's own item tooltip.
- Follows the item you pick to sell or buy at the auction house (the game's window or Auctionator's), including a
  name typed into the game's search box.

**Signals and analytics**
- Buy / Hold / Sell against each item's own history, Wait when sellers are dumping, Vendor when the auction
  house pays less than a vendor after the cut and the deposit, and DE when gear is worth more disenchanted than
  listed.
- Market phases: the week's price move read against supply (shortage, sellers withdrawing, dumping, ...).
- Volatility, trend, opportunity score, and an estimate of how many units sell per day.
- Sell-through: how much of what gets listed really sells, from real auction outcomes - yours and your
  guildmates' - and not only from scans, which can't tell a sale from an expired or cancelled listing. It corrects
  the sells per day, the Vendor and DE signals, values and posting prices for every item someone has listed.

**Posting prices**
- What to post at so it sells within the listing time, instead of undercutting by 1c: Quick, Normal and Patient
  prices from the listings ahead of you, how fast the item sells and how often it gets undercut.
- Learns each item's pattern from your scans and from how your own posts did, including how fast they actually
  sold (the moment a sale shows in chat, or when the auction mail arrives), and from how your guildmates' posts
  did; weighs the chance of selling against the deposit; never below the vendor price.
- On the item page, and in a bar under the auction house's sell window (the game's or Auctionator's): Quick,
  Normal, Patient and Market prices, the reason, the chance and time to sell and what you get after the cut; a
  click fills in the price. Click a listing in the sell window and the bar re-targets just under it. When a vendor
  pays more, it says so.
- Follows the newest scan: when a price moves during the day, the posting prices move with it.

**Arbitrage**
- Buy at one auction house and sell at another: your faction's and the goblin auction house, or between factions.
- Net profit after the real auction house cut and the listing deposit, supply and sell rate at both ends, and
  each side's data age; a sidebar breaks the selected route down.
- Market depth: after a search at the buy house, shows how many units stay profitable.
- Search button (or Shift-click) to look the item up at the auction house you're standing at.
- Routes paying over 2000% (gold moved between factions through junk listings, not trades) are left out and
  counted.

**Portfolio**
- What all your characters hold (bags, bank and active auctions; soulbound items left out), valued at your
  faction's market price - or, for items with a sell-through, at what they're really worth: the market price as
  often as they sell, the vendor's price otherwise.
- Average cost, unrealized profit, signal, sells per day and days to sell for each position.
- Ledger of your auction sales and purchases from auction mail, with the AH cut and realized profit. Sales show up
  as soon as the game reports them.
- Active auctions on all characters, and the total listed on the auction house right now.
- Customers and suppliers, when your auction mail names them.
- All characters or just this one; today, 7 days, 30 days or all time; export any table as text.

**Watchlists and alerts**
- Star items into any number of watchlists.
- Alerts below or above a price, or on Buy / Sell signals, checked after every scan; they show in chat and at the
  top of the screen, with a sound.

**Notifications**
- A bell left of the window's tabs keeps your alerts and sharing requests, with a count of the ones you haven't
  seen; click one to open its item or the sharing settings.
- Info bar addons (Titan Panel, ChocolateBar, Bazooka and others that read LibDataBroker) can show an "AME
  notifications" entry: the count, the newest on hover, and the list on a click.
- Share watchlists with your guild; guild members' lists appear in the sidebar.

**Shopping lists**
- Lists of what you're buying toward a goal - the reagents for a craft, say - with how many of each you need.
- Counts down from what all your characters hold in bags and bank, and shows who holds what.
- What's left to buy, priced at the market, with an optional top price per item (marked when the market is under
  it); search the auction house for the whole list in one go (with Auctionator).
- Add items from any item's right-click menu, or a recipe's reagents straight from the profession window.
- Share lists with your guild, and copy a guild member's list to your own.

**Sharing with your guild and friends**
- Full scans are shared automatically with guild members running AME, and relayed to players who were offline.
- The whole guild receives each scan at once, in small blocks: a block lost on the way is asked for again, a
  transfer cut off by a /reload carries on afterwards, and players who were away catch up on the last week's
  hourly prices first, then whole days. Settings, Sharing shows each guildmate you're receiving from, how far
  along it is and the time left. If sharing ever slows your game, the lighter v1 protocol is one setting away (Settings,
  Sharing).
- Sync now (Settings, Sharing) shares right away, and asks your guild and trusted players to send what they have.
- Your auction outcomes per item (sold, expired, cancelled - no prices, gold or buyers) are shared too, so
  everyone's sell-through and posting prices learn from everyone's listings.
- Trusted players outside the guild, with a request / approve handshake.
- Scans that disagree wildly with your prices are rejected.
- Big scans are packed and unpacked in small steps, so sending or receiving one doesn't freeze the game; in combat,
  what arrives is only set aside, and checked and stored once the fight ends.
- Personal blocklist, and a guild-wide blocklist set by officers in the guild information.
- How fresh each auction house's data is, and who scanned it.
- Players heard from: how many, on which protocol (v1 or v2), the most recent first, and the whole list by name.

**Tooltips**
- Three lines: your faction's market price against your chosen average, supply and sells per day, and the signal;
  a fourth with the sell-through when someone has listed the item.
- Hold Shift for the average, lowest price, 7-day change, supply trend, volatility, score, data confidence and age.

**Look and settings**
- A resizable window that remembers where you put it, with and without the auction house open, docks beside the
  auction house when there's room, and has its own scale (70-120%). Narrow windows get a compact layout.
- Window colours follow the market you're viewing (Horde red, Alliance blue, goblin gold), or a fixed theme; a
  colour-blind friendly palette (blue / orange) is one setting away.
- Settings in sections (General, Tooltips & charts, Selling, Sharing, Data). Minimap / broker button.
- The status row says what AME is working on (storing a search, updating the chart or market data) until what's on
  screen is current.
- Settings, Data shows what AME holds for each market: how far back its history goes, how many of the last week's
  hours have prices, the scans kept in full (yours and others'), and its size.
- A guide laid out like a documentation site: searchable articles in sections covering every feature, with AME's
  own elements (signals, the sell bar, a route, the chart legend) drawn in them; slash commands you can click to put
  in your chat box; and a glossary: right-click any explained column or number to read its entry.
- Optionally opens with the auction house (and closes with it), and switches to the Item tab for what you put up
  for sale.
- /amedev, for testing: measures what AME costs your game (and every fight, per addon) and sends it to AME's
  developer. Off until you type /amedev on; the guide's Commands page has the rest.
- In your client's language: English, German, French, Spanish, Russian, Portuguese, Italian, Korean and
  Chinese (simplified and traditional).

### New in this version (since 0.4.1-beta.7)

- **Notifications:** a bell left of the window's tabs keeps your alerts and sharing requests, with a count of the
  ones you haven't seen; click one to open its item or the sharing settings.
- **Info bars:** an "AME notifications" entry for info bar addons that read LibDataBroker (Titan Panel,
  ChocolateBar, Bazooka and others): how many you haven't seen, the newest on hover, the list on a click.
- **Posting prices follow the newest scan:** when an item's price moves during the day, the sell bar and the item
  page now price from today's market instead of the day's average, and cheap items get more room above the market.
- **Helping test:** /amedev on measures what AME costs your game, and every fight per addon, until /amedev off;
  /amedev send sends it to AME's developer. It does nothing until you turn it on.

**Fixes**
- Short freezes after a guildmate's full scan arrived: rebuilding the market data made the game clean up hundreds
  of megabytes of short-lived data. It now makes six times less, and reading saved prices is faster too (logins,
  charts and sync).
- Freezes of up to a third of a second on the Item and Market pages: names of random-suffix gear ("of the Monkey")
  were looked up hundreds at a time. They're now looked up a few at a time, and fill in over the next moments.
- The sell bar could show the same price for Quick, Normal and Patient, above today's market, after a price
  jumped.

### In 0.4.1-beta.7

- **Prices in silver and copper:** every box that takes a price - alerts, the Market price range, Arbitrage's
  minimum profit - reads 12, 1.5, 12g, 9s 50c, 1g 20s or 150c (and the game's own coin letters in your language).
  Hover a box for the forms; text it can't read turns red.
- **The guide** explains MA 20 and the 7-day average under their own heading on the Item page.

**Fixes**
- Sync could stall for a guildmate after a disconnect: a transfer that lost blocks waited until the sender had
  nothing else to send, which with a busy guild could be hours, and Sync now didn't help. Lost blocks are now asked
  for within a minute, resent ahead of a long transfer, and an object that can't be finished is asked for again.
- An alert under 1 gold showed as 0 in its box.

### In 0.4.1-beta.6

- **Smoother play:** AME's background work (rebuilding market data, storing what your guild sends) waits until a
  fight ends, and the work after a login runs in small steps instead of freezing the game for up to a second.
- **Catching up over sync:** a guildmate who was away gets the week's hourly prices before any whole day, so the
  7D chart fills in first. Settings, Sharing shows one line per guildmate sending to you, with the time left.
- **The status row** (bottom left of the window) says what AME is working on - storing a search, updating the
  chart, market data or your guild's data - until the page is current.

**Fixes**
- Sync turned down most of what it received: anything big enough to be unpacked in steps (most full scans, whole
  days) was rejected as unreadable. Everything received is stored now.
- After a search of the item on screen (putting it in the sell tab, say), its chart re-read two days of scans,
  which kept AME busy for seconds: it now adds just the new search.

### In 0.4.1-beta.5

- **MA 20 on the item chart:** a moving average over the last 20 candles, as trading apps draw it, beside the
  7-day average. Click a line in the legend to hide it or show it again; AME remembers.
- **Your data:** Settings, Data shows what AME holds for each market.
- **Big guilds:** Players heard from (Settings, Sharing) sums up how many players share, how many on v2 and v1,
  and who was heard in the last hour; each player shows their protocol, and Show all lists everyone by name.
- **The game's auction house window:** a name typed into its search box opens that item on the Item tab, and so
  does an item put in its sell slot.

**Fixes**
- The 7D chart only showed the days since hourly prices began: it now spans the whole week (older days one point
  a day).
- The 7-day average line jumped with single scans and stopped short of the right edge: it now rolls hour by hour
  and runs up to the newest data.
- Supply bars line up with the candles, one per candle.
- Listing deposits: AME counted at least 1 silver per post, but the game charges the plain rate (60c for two
  Medium Leather). Cheap items no longer get a wrong "vendor it instead".
- Players who only shared over v2 didn't appear under Players heard from.

### In 0.4.1-beta.4

- **Full scan button:** without a scanning addon, the auction house window gets a Full scan button in its title
  bar. It shows the scan's progress beside it and counts down to the next full scan the game allows (one every 15
  minutes). /ame scan does the same, with or without a scanning addon.
- **Sync v2:** guild sharing sends each scan to the whole guild at once instead of to one player at a time, in
  small blocks that are asked for again when lost and carry on after a /reload. Players who were away catch up on
  a week of hourly prices. Guild members still on an older version keep getting scans the old way. The lighter v1
  protocol stays available under Settings, Sharing, if sharing slows your game.
- **Hourly prices for a week:** the 7D chart now shows hourly prices, from your scans and your guild's.
- **Vendor price on the item chart:** a dashed line when it's close to the auction house prices, otherwise a note
  in the legend.
- **Quicker loading screen:** AME's biggest saved tables load in the background after you log in, out of combat.
  The first logout on this version saves them in the new form; logins after that are quicker.

**Fixes**
- Market overview items showed as "item:12345" until hovered: their names now fill in on their own.

### In 0.4.1-beta.3

**Fixes**
- The Crafting sidebar's button showed up empty: it now says what it does ("Add all reagents to a shopping
  list", "Search the AH"), and on Leveling it only appears once you pick a step.

### In 0.4.1-beta.2

**Fixes**
- The Crafting setting stayed hidden even with its data addon installed, so the Crafting tab could not be
  switched on.

### In 0.4.1-beta.1

- **Sell-through:** scans see listings disappear but not why, so an item reposted every day that never sells looked
  like thousands sold a day. AME now counts what really happened to auctions - sales and expired items from your
  auction mail, and auctions you cancel - and shares it with your guild. Where someone has listed an item, its
  sells per day, Vendor and DE signals, portfolio value and posting prices follow what actually sold. The tooltip
  shows it ("sell-through 14%  2 of 14 sold"); see Sell-through in the guide's glossary.
- **Sync now:** a button under Settings, Sharing that shares right away instead of within the next 10 minutes.
  Guild members answer it once they're on this version.

**Fixes**
- Random-suffix gear: lists show the full name ("... of the Eagle"), tooltips show that suffix's stats, and
  searching the auction house for it (from Arbitrage, shopping lists or the item page) looks for that exact item.
- No more "blocked action" error when a guild member logs on or off while you're in combat.
