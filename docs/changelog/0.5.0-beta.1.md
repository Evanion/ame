## Azeroth Mercantile Exchange 0.5.0-beta.1

AME turns the auction house into a trading desk: price history, charts, signals, arbitrage, posting prices and your
own portfolio. It records every scan and search you run through the game's native auction house APIs, so it works
with the default auction house window and any scanning addon (it works well with addons like Auctionator); without
one, a Full scan button on the auction house window runs a full scan for you. It never scans on its own and needs
no desktop app.

### Everything in AME so far

**Price history**
- Records full scans (replicate, browse-summary and classic "get all" scans), item searches, sell-tab rechecks and
  category browsing.
- Separate history per market (realm, or ruleset on WoW Forever) and per auction house: Horde, Alliance and goblin.
- Compact storage: full detail for the last 48 hours, hourly prices for a week, daily history for 60 days, weekly
  points for a year.
- A Full scan button on the auction house window when no scanning addon is loaded (and /ame scan with or without
  one), with its progress beside it and a countdown to the next scan the game allows (one every 15 minutes).
  Settings, Data makes it Detailed (every listing, once per 15 minutes) or Fast (each item's cheapest listing, any
  time).
- Scans that only see each item's cheapest listing (browse-summary scans) get their market price from how far above
  the cheapest each item usually sells, so they don't drag the market line down or flatten the candles' wicks.
- Optional one-time import of your existing Auctionator price history.
- Outrageous prices (e.g. a single listing at a 1000% markup) are kept out of the numbers.
- Gear with a random suffix ("of the Monkey", "of Magic") is tracked per suffix, since each sells at its own price,
  shown with its full name and its own stats, and searched for by its full name.

**Market screener**
- Every item with history, with market price, how it compares with its average (7 days by default), supply, sells
  per day, signal and an opportunity score; optional columns for the average, lowest price, 7-day change,
  volatility, data age and market phase (column chooser).
- Auction house categories and watchlists in a sidebar, name search, quality filter (grey junk hidden by default),
  price range (in gold, or silver and copper: "9s 50c") and signal filters, with removable filter chips and saved views. Every column sorts.
- Rows with data older than 3 days fade; hovering a row explains its signal.
- Data confidence bars on every item.
- Market selector for any auction house you have data for.
- Right-click any item in a list (Market, Arbitrage, Portfolio) for its menu: open the chart, search the auction
  house, watchlists, an alert, a chat link, or hide it.
- Hide items you never trade: they leave the Market, Arbitrage and Portfolio lists and their totals (AME keeps
  recording them), and wait under Hidden in the sidebar to be unhidden.

**Market overview** (switch it on in Settings, General)
- The whole auction house at a glance, like a stock exchange's indexes: a market index and a CPI (what players use
  up, weighted by what's spent on it), listed value, turnover, auctions and breadth, over the period you pick.
- Categories by share of the listed value (click one to open it in the screener), the biggest risers and fallers
  per category, and the items the total leans on most.
- Fair numbers: only items listed at least 3 at a time on most days move the indexes, and prices are capped at the
  outlier limit. The largest items show how many are listed and the price each, and what a capped one came to
  uncapped.

**Item charts**
- Price chart with market, lowest and 7-day average, or candlesticks with daily highs and lows (the Guide shows how
  to read them).
- 2D intraday view (every scan of the last two days), a 7D view with hourly prices, and 30D to All ranges. An item
  opens on 2D when it has a recent scan (7D otherwise) and stays on that range as new prices come in.
- Every line, candle and bar is merged into the same time slots, about 60 across what's on screen and smaller as
  you zoom in, so hovering a slot reads them all.
- Two averages, as on a trading chart: MA 20 (the last 20 slots, so its span follows the zoom) and the fixed
  7-day average the signals compare against. Click any line in the legend to hide or show it.
- The vendor price as a dashed line when it's close to the auction house prices (otherwise noted in the legend).
- Your own purchases and sales as dots on the chart.
- Zoom, pan, value tags and a hover readout.
- Search by item name, Shift-click items while the Item tab is showing, or pick a modifier click in Settings.
- Hover the item's name or icon for the game's own item tooltip.
- Follows the item you pick to sell or buy at the auction house (the game's window or Auctionator's), including a
  name typed into the game's search box.

**Signals and analytics**
- Buy / Hold / Sell against each item's own history, Wait when sellers are dumping, Vendor when the auction
  house pays less than a vendor after the cut and the deposit, and DE when gear is worth more disenchanted than
  listed.
- Market phases: the week's price move read against supply (shortage, sellers withdrawing, dumping, ...).
- Volatility, trend, opportunity score, and an estimate of how many units sell per day.
- Sell-through: how much of what gets listed really sells, from real auction outcomes - yours and your
  guildmates' - and not only from scans, which can't tell a sale from an expired or cancelled listing. It corrects
  the sells per day, the Vendor and DE signals, values and posting prices for every item someone has listed.

**Posting prices**
- What to post at so it sells within the listing time, instead of undercutting by 1c, from the listings ahead of
  you, how fast the item sells, how often it gets undercut and how far its price swings in a day: Quick goes under
  where the price usually dips within a few hours, Normal is the best price for the chance, Patient waits for the
  usual peak within the listing time. Quick only shows when it's cheaper than Normal.
- Priced from the listings you just searched, at the same sells per day every other view shows.
- Learns each item's pattern from your scans and from how your own posts did, including how fast they actually
  sold (the moment a sale shows in chat, or when the auction mail arrives), and from how your guildmates' posts
  did; weighs the chance of selling against the deposit; never below the vendor price.
- On the item page, and in a bar under the auction house's sell window (the game's or Auctionator's): Quick,
  Normal, Patient and Market prices, the reason, the chance and time to sell and what you get after the cut; a
  click fills in the price. Click a listing in the sell window and the bar re-targets just under it. When a vendor
  pays more, it says so.
- Follows the newest scan: when a price moves during the day, the posting prices move with it.

**Arbitrage**
- Buy at one auction house and sell at another: your faction's and the goblin auction house, or between factions.
- Net profit after the real auction house cut and the listing deposit, supply and sell rate at both ends, and
  each side's data age; a sidebar breaks the selected route down.
- Market depth: after a search at the buy house, shows how many units stay profitable.
- Search button (or Shift-click) to look the item up at the auction house you're standing at.
- A Columns button to hide the columns you don't use.
- Routes paying over 2000% (gold moved between factions through junk listings, not trades) are left out and
  counted.

**Portfolio**
- What all your characters hold (bags, bank, mailbox, guild banks and active auctions; soulbound items left out),
  valued at what selling it nets at your faction's auction house after the cut and deposit - as often as it really
  sells, for items with a sell-through - or at a vendor's price when that pays more.
- Average cost, unrealized profit, signal, sells per day and days to sell for each position.
- Ledger of your auction sales and purchases from auction mail, with the AH cut and realized profit. Sales show up
  as soon as the game reports them.
- Active auctions on all characters, and the total listed on the auction house right now.
- Customers and suppliers, when your auction mail names them.
- All characters or just this one; today, 7 days, 30 days or all time; export any table as text.

**Watchlists and alerts**
- Star items into any number of watchlists.
- Alerts below or above a price, or on Buy / Sell signals, checked after every scan; they show in chat and at the
  top of the screen, with a sound.

**Notifications**
- A bell left of the window's tabs keeps your alerts and sharing requests, with a count of the ones you haven't
  seen; click one to open its item or the sharing settings.
- Info bar addons (Titan Panel, ChocolateBar, Bazooka and others that read LibDataBroker) can show an "AME
  notifications" entry: the count, the newest on hover, and the list on a click.
- Share watchlists with your guild; guild members' lists appear in the sidebar.

**Shopping lists**
- Lists of what you're buying toward a goal - the reagents for a craft, say - with how many of each you need.
- Counts down from what your characters on this realm hold in bags, bank, mailbox and guild banks, and shows who
  holds what.
- What's left to buy, priced at the market, with an optional top price per item (marked when the market is under
  it); search the auction house for the whole list in one go (with Auctionator, or the Shopping tab below).
- Items a vendor sells are marked as vendor buys and left out of the auction house searches; the vendor panel buys
  them.
- Add items from any item's right-click menu, or a recipe's reagents straight from the profession window.
- Share lists with your guild, and copy a guild member's list to your own.

**Auction house tabs** (when Auctionator isn't loaded)
- Shopping: search the auction house with filters (category, required and item level, quality, price each, exact
  name, usable by you), with the Market screener's columns and signals for every result; recent searches and your
  shopping lists on the left, Search all for a list. Click an item for its listings; buying a stackable highlights
  the listings the order takes and warns when the game's price is over them (Settings, Buying); each listing shows
  its time left. Shift-click an item to search it. What you buy counts as in your mailbox right away.
- Selling: what in your bags can be listed, worth the most first, with a tip for each (Sell, Arbitrate - make
  something worth more first - DE or vendor; sort by it); pick one for AME's posting prices (Quick, Normal,
  Patient, or the market price), the deposit and what you get, and post. What's on sale now shows beside it, yours
  marked; click a listing to price just under it.

**Profession window**
- Profit on every recipe in the game's recipe list ("no price" when a product or reagent has none; hover for the
  breakdown), and a bar under the window for the picked one: cost to craft, what it sells for and the profit, its
  sells per day and signal, for as many as you'll make, with what's left to buy.
- Craft queue: parts first, each material's source (bank, an alt, a guild bank, the mailbox, a vendor, made in the
  queue, or the auction house), make or buy for each part, a to-do list, and Craft next. Missing to shopping list
  puts what has to be bought on a list.
- Who can make it: your guild's and trusted players' recipes are shared over sync; find a crafter by item or
  enchant, or hold Shift on a craftable item's tooltip. The recipe items that teach each recipe are listed with
  their auction house price, and how many players' recipes AME knows.
- Where to get a recipe: vendors and drops, learned as you and your guild play, on the recipe item's tooltip.
- An item a vendor pays more for, when a recipe you know makes it worth more (silk cloth into bandages): the item
  page says to make that first.

**Crafting tab** (with the AME Crafting Data addon, installed with AME)
- Pages in a sidebar, with shared filters: Top earners (sorted by profit per craft, with each product's signal;
  Queue a craft, or put its reagents on a shopping list), Sell or refine (whether smelting or crafting beats selling
  raw; Queue the smelt), Recipes to buy (on the auction house, profit per craft; Usable only keeps what the
  character you're on can learn now), Your professions (and the ones AME hasn't recorded yet, with what to do),
  Find a crafter (one row per crafted thing; everyone who can make it, then the recipe item that teaches it with
  its price; Whisper each crafter),
  the Craft queue (steps with Buy or Make for each part, remove a craft, a To do list in order, profit and what's
  left to buy) and Leveling (the cheapest path to a skill, searchable; Queue the steps, or its reagents to a list).
- Market/day: what the whole market makes crafting it a day, not just you.
- Counts what your characters on this realm hold in bags, bank, mailbox and guild banks, and makes a part
  yourself when that's cheaper than buying it.

**At a vendor**
- When a vendor sells something your shopping lists still need, a panel beside it shows how many you need, what
  that buys in the vendor's stacks and the cost: Buy per item, or Buy all.

**Sharing with your guild and friends**
- Full scans are shared automatically with guild members running AME, and relayed to players who were offline.
- The whole guild receives each scan at once, in small blocks: a block lost on the way is asked for again, a
  transfer cut off by a /reload carries on afterwards, and players who were away catch up on the last week's
  hourly prices first, then whole days. If sharing ever slows your game, the lighter v1 protocol is one setting away
  (Settings, Sharing).
- Sync activity (Settings, Sharing): when the next announce goes out, what's being sent and received with a
  progress bar and the time left each (and when one isn't moving, why and for how long, with Retry and Cancel),
  and Missing here - what each player announced that you don't have yet, per
  auction house, with its size, how long it takes to arrive and whether it's on its way. Sync now on it shares right
  away, and asks your guild and trusted players to send what they have.
- Your auction outcomes per item (sold, expired, cancelled - no prices, gold or buyers) are shared too, so
  everyone's sell-through and posting prices learn from everyone's listings.
- Trusted players outside the guild, with a request / approve handshake.
- Scans that disagree wildly with your prices are rejected.
- Big scans are packed and unpacked in small steps, so sending or receiving one doesn't freeze the game; in combat,
  what arrives is only set aside, and checked and stored once the fight ends.
- Personal blocklist, and a guild-wide blocklist set by officers in the guild information.
- How fresh each auction house's data is, and who scanned it.
- Players heard from: how many, which AME version and protocol (v1 or v2) each runs, the most recent first, and the
  whole list by name.
- When someone you share with has a newer version of AME, it says so once in chat and in the notification center,
  and the window's footer recommends updating.

**Tooltips**
- One block under the game's lines: your faction's auction house and how fresh its price is, the market price each
  and for all you have (and what a vendor pays), the disenchant value on gear, what you have and where (bags, bank,
  mailbox and guild banks, on all your characters), what you have up on the auction house, what the ones in your
  bags and bank are worth, how it compares with its average, how many sell a day and its sell-through, a notice
  when the goblin auction house pays more after its cut, and the signal with its reason (what price and supply are
  doing, or how much disenchanting or a vendor beats listing it).
- Hold Shift for what to post it at (with the chance it sells, and the deposit on gear), what it disenchants into,
  yours per character and guild bank with your average cost, every auction house after its cut (with the goblin
  auction house's sells per day and how old its data is, and where an alt of yours could sell it), the price and
  data details, and who can make it.
- Compact on small screens.

**Other addons**
- Baganator: icon corners for AME's signal (BUY and SELL; HOLD and WAIT if you ask), what the stack is worth (in
  the coin's colour) and the tip (what to do with it, as the Selling tab says); and a sort, AME: worth, the most
  valuable stacks first. Settings, General, Other addons says what the corners show, or turns it all off. What's
  junk is Baganator's own.
- The launcher works from the minimap button, info bar addons (LibDataBroker) and the game's addon menu.
- For addon authors and WeakAuras: AzerothMercantileExchange.API.v1 gives AME's market price, worth, signal, item
  details and posting prices for an item, and tells you when new prices are in.

**Look and settings**
- A resizable window that remembers where you put it, with and without the auction house open, docks beside the
  auction house when there's room, and has its own scale (70-120%). Narrow windows get a compact layout.
- Window colours follow the market you're viewing (Horde red, Alliance blue, goblin gold), or a fixed theme; a
  colour-blind friendly palette (blue / orange) is one setting away.
- Settings in sections (General, Tooltips & charts, Selling, Sharing, Data), behind a cog by the bell. Minimap /
  broker button.
- The status row says what AME is working on (storing a search, updating the chart or market data) until what's on
  screen is current.
- Settings, Data shows what AME holds for each market: how far back its history goes, how many of the last week's
  hours have prices, the scans kept in full (yours and others'), and its size.
- A guide laid out like a documentation site: searchable articles in sections covering every feature, with AME's
  own elements (signals, the sell bar, a route, the chart legend) drawn in them; slash commands you can click to put
  in your chat box; and a glossary: right-click any explained column or number to read its entry.
- Optionally opens with the auction house, and switches to the Item tab for what you put up for sale. Opened at the
  auction house, it closes with it; open before, it stays open.
- /amedev, for testing: measures what AME costs your game (and every fight, per addon) and sends it to AME's
  developer. Off until you type /amedev on; the guide's Commands page has the rest. Not getting prices from your
  guild? /amedev sync shows why, and /amedev send sends it to AME's developer.
- In your client's language: English, German, French, Spanish, Russian, Portuguese, Italian, Korean and
  Chinese (simplified and traditional).

- At the auction house the new window takes over from the game's (Settings can keep the game's, with AME beside

- Sell: your bags at full height; the posting card and what's on sale now beside them; the item's chart under them
  with its spans from 2 days to all.
- Market: the screener, the overview, arbitrage and your lists in one tab, opening on the mode you used last.
- Item: search suggestions as you type; the chart, prices, buying and posting in one page.
- Guild: craft requests with their exchange (ask, bring your own reagents, the crafter answers, you accept),

- Crafting: what you can make now, the queue, what's missing, recipes to learn, leveling and your professions.

  now includes; every setting explained on hover; one notification sound, picked from LibSharedMedia's list.

Fixes in both windows:
- The crafting tools card no longer errors when one of your characters holds the tool.
- A chart's first and last day labels stay inside the chart.
- Lighter to keep open: the new window draws a screen again only when what it shows changes (your gold, bags,
  mail, a new scan, your recipes), and Home keeps what it worked out until then instead of every 30 seconds.
- Baganator's corners work out "make something of it instead" once for your whole bag, not once per item, and the
  background work pauses within it, so a bag of vendor items with many known recipes doesn't hold up a frame.
- Before any profession was recorded, AME worked out recipe data again on every look-up; now it's kept.
