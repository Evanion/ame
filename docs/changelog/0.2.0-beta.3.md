## Azeroth Mercantile Exchange 0.2.0-beta.3

AME turns the auction house into a trading desk: price history, charts, signals, arbitrage, posting prices and your
own portfolio. It records every scan and search you run through the game's native auction house APIs, so it works
with the default auction house window and any scanning addon (it works well with addons like Auctionator). It never
scans on its own and needs no desktop app.

### Everything in AME so far

**Price history**
- Records full scans (replicate, browse-summary and classic "get all" scans), item searches, sell-tab rechecks and
  category browsing.
- Separate history per market (realm, or ruleset on WoW Forever) and per auction house: Horde, Alliance and goblin.
- Compact storage: full detail for the last 48 hours, daily history for 60 days, weekly points for a year.
- Optional one-time import of your existing Auctionator price history.
- Outrageous prices (e.g. a single listing at a 1000% markup) are kept out of the numbers.

**Market screener**
- Every item with history, with market price, average (7 days by default), 7-day change, volatility, supply, sells
  per day, signal and an opportunity score.
- Auction house categories, name search, quality filter (grey junk hidden by default), price range and signal
  filters. Every column sorts.
- Data confidence bars on every item.
- Market selector for any auction house you have data for.

**Item charts**
- Price chart with market, lowest and 7-day average, or candlesticks with daily highs and lows.
- 2D intraday view (every scan of the last two days), and 7D to All ranges.
- Your own purchases and sales as dots on the chart.
- Zoom, pan, value tags and a hover readout.
- Search by item name, Shift-click items while the window is open, or pick a modifier click in Settings.
- Follows the item you pick to sell or buy at the auction house (the game's window or Auctionator's).

**Signals and analytics**
- Buy / Hold / Sell against each item's own history, Wait when sellers are dumping, and Vendor when the auction
  house pays less than a vendor after the cut and the deposit.
- Market phases: the week's price move read against supply (shortage, sellers withdrawing, dumping, ...).
- Volatility, trend, opportunity score, and an estimate of how many units sell per day.

**Posting prices**
- What to post at so it sells within the listing time, instead of undercutting by 1c: Quick, Normal and Patient
  prices from the listings ahead of you, how fast the item sells and how often it gets undercut.
- Learns each item's pattern from your scans and from how your own posts did; weighs the chance of selling
  against the deposit; never below the vendor price.
- On the item page, and in a bar under the auction house's sell window: a click fills in the price.

**Arbitrage**
- Buy at one auction house and sell at another: your faction's and the goblin auction house, or between factions.
- Includes the real auction house cut and listing deposits, supply and sell rate at both ends, and data age.
- Market depth: after a search at the buy house, shows how many units stay profitable.
- Search button (or Shift-click) to look the item up at the auction house you're standing at.

**Portfolio**
- What all your characters hold (bags, bank and active auctions; soulbound items left out), valued at your
  faction's market price.
- Average cost, unrealized profit, signal, sells per day and days to sell for each position.
- Ledger of your auction sales and purchases from auction mail, with the AH cut and realized profit. Sales show up
  as soon as the game reports them.
- Active auctions on all characters, and the total listed on the auction house right now.
- Customers and suppliers, when your auction mail names them.

**Watchlists and alerts**
- Star items into any number of watchlists.
- Alerts below or above a price, or on Buy / Sell signals, checked after every scan.
- Share watchlists with your guild; guild members' lists appear in the sidebar.

**Sharing with your guild and friends**
- Full scans are shared automatically with guild members running AME, and relayed to players who were offline.
- Trusted players outside the guild, with a request / approve handshake.
- Scans that disagree wildly with your prices are rejected.
- Personal blocklist, and a guild-wide blocklist set by officers in the guild information.

**Tooltips**
- Your faction's market price and 7-day change, and the average over your chosen period.
- Hold Shift for lowest price, supply, sells per day, volatility, score, data confidence and data age.

**Look and settings**
- Window colours follow the market you're viewing (Horde red, Alliance blue, goblin gold), or a fixed theme.
- Settings and guide tabs, hover explanations on every column, minimap / broker button.
- Optionally opens with the auction house (and closes with it).
- In your client's language: English, German, French, Spanish, Russian, Portuguese, Italian, Korean and
  Chinese (simplified and traditional).

### New in this version (since 0.2.0-beta.2)

- **Posting prices:** Quick / Normal / Patient suggestions on the item page and in a bar under the AH sell window.
- **Vendor signal** for items the auction house pays less for than a vendor.
- **Your trades on the chart** as dots.
- **The Item tab follows** the item you pick to sell or buy at the auction house.
- **Open AME with the auction house**, and optionally close it with it (Settings).
- **Faster and smoother:**
  - market data is saved between sessions, so it's there right after login;
  - rebuilding it after scans is about three times faster and runs in the background while the old data stays on
    screen ("updating market data...");
  - storing a full scan no longer freezes a frame;
  - the window is built in idle moments after login, so opening it doesn't stutter;
  - picking an item in the sell window reacts right away;
  - the Portfolio ledger opens instantly.
- **Smaller saved data:** daily history for 60 days, then weekly for a year.
- **Chart clicks are off by default**, because loot addons such as Gargul use modifier clicks on items and links:
  Shift-click with the window open, or choose Alt / Alt+Shift / Ctrl+Alt-click in Settings.
- **Cleaner look:** buttons size to their (translated) labels, ticked checkboxes are clearly filled, the Guide
  scrolls, summary cards line up, the footer shows just the version.
- **One place for settings:** the game's AddOns options page now opens AME's Settings tab.
- Soulbound items don't count as stock.
- Fixed: auction mail could be recorded twice in the ledger.
- Fixed: "auction sold" messages weren't recognised in some languages.
- Fixed: 43 texts were never translated; Settings descriptions could overlap the next button.
- Errors are now reported once in chat and in `/ame status` instead of looking like missing data.
