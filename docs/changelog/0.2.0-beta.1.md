## Azeroth Mercantile Exchange 0.2.0-beta.1

AME records auction house prices from every scan and search you run, through the game's native auction house APIs.
It works with the default auction house window and any scanning addon (it works well with addons like Auctionator).

### New

- **Portfolio tab.** What all your characters hold (bags, bank and active auctions), valued at your faction's market
  price, with average cost, unrealized profit, Buy / Hold / Sell, sells per day and days to sell.
  - **Ledger:** your auction sales and purchases from auction mail, with the AH cut and realized profit per sale.
    A sale shows up as soon as the game reports it, and the mail fills in the details.
  - **Auctions:** every active auction on your characters, and the total value listed on the AH right now.
  - **Customers / Suppliers:** who buys from you and who you buy from, when your auction mail names them.
- **Sells per day.** An estimate of how fast each item moves, from supply draining between scans. It's in the
  screener, on the item page, in Shift tooltips and on the Arbitrage tab.
- **Market depth on Arbitrage.** After you search an item at the buy auction house, AME shows how many units the
  route stays profitable for, e.g. "50 +81s".
- **Data confidence.** Bars after each item show how much to trust its numbers, from the number of recent scans and
  how fresh they are.
- **Guild watchlists.** Right-click a watchlist to share it with your guild. Guild members' lists appear in the
  sidebar, and you can copy them.
- **Market phases.** AME reads the week's price move against supply: shortage, steady demand, sellers piling in,
  tightening, balanced, pressure building, sellers withdrawing, weak demand or dumping. A Buy while dumping becomes
  **Wait**.

### Changed

- All lists use one table: every column sorts, and item names fill in by themselves.
- Signals sort Sell, Buy, Hold, Wait.

### Fixed

- Gear prices from item searches were divided by the number of identical auctions.
