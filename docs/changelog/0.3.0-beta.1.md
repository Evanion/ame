## Azeroth Mercantile Exchange 0.3.0-beta.1

AME turns the auction house into a trading desk: price history, charts, signals, arbitrage, posting prices and your
own portfolio. It records every scan and search you run through the game's native auction house APIs, so it works
with the default auction house window and any scanning addon (it works well with addons like Auctionator). It never
scans on its own and needs no desktop app.

### Everything in AME so far

**Price history**
- Records full scans (replicate, browse-summary and classic "get all" scans), item searches, sell-tab rechecks and
  category browsing.
- Separate history per market (realm, or ruleset on WoW Forever) and per auction house: Horde, Alliance and goblin.
- Compact storage: full detail for the last 48 hours, daily history for 60 days, weekly points for a year.
- Optional one-time import of your existing Auctionator price history.
- Outrageous prices (e.g. a single listing at a 1000% markup) are kept out of the numbers.

**Market screener**
- Every item with history, with market price, how it compares with its average (7 days by default), supply, sells
  per day, signal and an opportunity score; optional columns for the average, lowest price, 7-day change,
  volatility, data age and market phase (column chooser).
- Auction house categories and watchlists in a sidebar, name search, quality filter (grey junk hidden by default),
  price range and signal filters, with removable filter chips and saved views. Every column sorts.
- Rows with data older than 3 days fade; hovering a row explains its signal.
- Data confidence bars on every item.
- Market selector for any auction house you have data for.

**Item charts**
- Price chart with market, lowest and 7-day average, or candlesticks with daily highs and lows.
- 2D intraday view (every scan of the last two days), and 7D to All ranges.
- Your own purchases and sales as dots on the chart.
- Zoom, pan, value tags and a hover readout.
- Search by item name, Shift-click items while the window is open, or pick a modifier click in Settings.
- Follows the item you pick to sell or buy at the auction house (the game's window or Auctionator's).

**Signals and analytics**
- Buy / Hold / Sell against each item's own history, Wait when sellers are dumping, and Vendor when the auction
  house pays less than a vendor after the cut and the deposit.
- Market phases: the week's price move read against supply (shortage, sellers withdrawing, dumping, ...).
- Volatility, trend, opportunity score, and an estimate of how many units sell per day.

**Posting prices**
- What to post at so it sells within the listing time, instead of undercutting by 1c: Quick, Normal and Patient
  prices from the listings ahead of you, how fast the item sells and how often it gets undercut.
- Learns each item's pattern from your scans and from how your own posts did, including how fast they actually
  sold (the moment a sale shows in chat, or when the auction mail arrives); weighs the chance of selling against
  the deposit; never below the vendor price.
- On the item page, and in a bar under the auction house's sell window (the game's or Auctionator's): Quick,
  Normal, Patient and Market prices, the reason, the chance and time to sell and what you get after the cut; a
  click fills in the price. Click a listing in the sell window and the bar re-targets just under it. When a vendor
  pays more, it says so.

**Arbitrage**
- Buy at one auction house and sell at another: your faction's and the goblin auction house, or between factions.
- Net profit after the real auction house cut and the listing deposit, supply and sell rate at both ends, and
  each side's data age; a sidebar breaks the selected route down.
- Market depth: after a search at the buy house, shows how many units stay profitable.
- Search button (or Shift-click) to look the item up at the auction house you're standing at.
- Routes paying over 2000% (gold moved between factions through junk listings, not trades) are left out and
  counted.

**Portfolio**
- What all your characters hold (bags, bank and active auctions; soulbound items left out), valued at your
  faction's market price.
- Average cost, unrealized profit, signal, sells per day and days to sell for each position.
- Ledger of your auction sales and purchases from auction mail, with the AH cut and realized profit. Sales show up
  as soon as the game reports them.
- Active auctions on all characters, and the total listed on the auction house right now.
- Customers and suppliers, when your auction mail names them.
- All characters or just this one; today, 7 days, 30 days or all time; export any table as text.

**Watchlists and alerts**
- Star items into any number of watchlists.
- Alerts below or above a price, or on Buy / Sell signals, checked after every scan.
- Share watchlists with your guild; guild members' lists appear in the sidebar.

**Sharing with your guild and friends**
- Full scans are shared automatically with guild members running AME, and relayed to players who were offline.
- Trusted players outside the guild, with a request / approve handshake.
- Scans that disagree wildly with your prices are rejected.
- Personal blocklist, and a guild-wide blocklist set by officers in the guild information.
- How fresh each auction house's data is, and who scanned it.

**Tooltips**
- Three lines: your faction's market price against your chosen average, supply and sells per day, and the signal.
- Hold Shift for the average, lowest price, 7-day change, supply trend, volatility, score, data confidence and age.

**Look and settings**
- A resizable window that remembers where you put it, with and without the auction house open, docks beside the
  auction house when there's room, and has its own scale (70-120%). Narrow windows get a compact layout.
- Window colours follow the market you're viewing (Horde red, Alliance blue, goblin gold), or a fixed theme; a
  colour-blind friendly palette (blue / orange) is one setting away.
- Settings in sections (General, Tooltips & charts, Selling, Sharing, Data). Minimap / broker button.
- A guide laid out like a documentation site: searchable articles in sections, with AME's own elements (signals,
  the sell bar, a route, the chart legend) drawn in them; slash commands you can click to put in your chat box; and
  a glossary: right-click any explained column or number to read its entry.
- Optionally opens with the auction house (and closes with it), and switches to the Item tab for what you put up
  for sale.
- In your client's language: English, German, French, Spanish, Russian, Portuguese, Italian, Korean and
  Chinese (simplified and traditional).

### New in this version (since 0.2.0-beta.4)

**A redesign of every screen.** AME had grown from a price recorder into a trading desk inside a fixed, cramped
window. Now:

- **The window resizes** (drag the bottom-right corner), remembers its size and place separately with and without
  the auction house open, docks beside the auction house when there's room, and has its own scale. Below 900 px
  wide, tabs switch to a compact layout. Every tab's sidebar has the same width and place; the footer belongs to
  the window and says where each tab's numbers come from.
- **Item tab:** the chart fills the space, with its ranges and Line | Candles on the chart and the legend in a
  corner. A sidebar gives the verdict in plain words (market price, change, signal, data confidence), key numbers,
  what to post at and what you'd get, and watch and alerts. The hover readout is in aligned columns. Compact: a
  one-line summary and a Details button.
- **Sell window bar:** Quick, Normal, Patient and Market prices (one lit at a time), the reason on its own line,
  confidence bars, the chance and time to sell, and your net after the cut. Works on Auctionator's selling tab too.
- **Market:** a category sidebar, filter chips, a signal filter with icons (up and down triangles, a dot, a pause
  sign and a coin, so signals never rely on colour alone; the picked one fills with its colour), column chooser,
  saved views, faded stale rows, and signal reasons on hover.
- **Arbitrage:** a Net column after the cut and the deposit, a sidebar for the selected route with the full
  breakdown, each side's data age and a Search button for the buy house. Gold transfers between factions (routes
  over 2000%) are hidden and counted.
- **Portfolio:** all characters or this one, periods (today, 7 / 30 days, all time), and Export to text (or as
  spreadsheet columns).
- **Settings** has a sidebar of sections. **Sync is now the Sharing section of Settings** (`/ame config`), with how
  fresh each auction house's data is and who scanned it. Your own characters never show up in its lists.
- **Guide:** searchable articles in sections, with AME's own elements drawn in them, and clickable slash commands.
- **Posting prices learn how fast your listings sell:** each sale (seen in chat, or from the auction mail) and each
  expiry tunes that item's estimate.
- **Colour-blind friendly palette**, and selected buttons readable on every theme (contrast checked).
- **Tooltips** are three lines by default; Shift shows the rest.
- Gold amounts are shown in coin colours; large quantities are shortened (4.5k); "Sales/day" replaces "Sells/d".
- Full scans show their real progress; empty tabs say what to do next; one-time tips on the sell bar and the
  Arbitrage tab.

**Fixes**
- The window no longer grows each time you grab its resize corner.
- Sorting the Market list by item name no longer empties it.
- Cards and text no longer overflow on the Settings and Sharing pages.
- Sharing no longer lists you as another player (WoW Forever names message senders "Name Suffix").
